# Profile: pure

First-principles / derivation bundle. Optimize for *understanding and generality* over a number.

- **Primary skills:** `symmetry-and-conservation-laws` and `model-building-and-approximation`
  lead; the branch skills derive results from stated laws and assumptions.
- **Derive before computing.** State the governing law, the symmetry/conservation structure,
  and the regime of validity before any worked number.
- **Every result is checked against a limiting case** (`limiting-cases-and-asymptotics`):
  the non-relativistic ($v\ll c$), classical ($\hbar\to0$), weak-field, or thermodynamic
  limit must recover the known result.
- **Assumptions and idealizations are surfaced**, with the regime where the model breaks.
- Output ends with the skill's **checkable ledger** (conserved quantities, invariants,
  limit checks) — and, borrowing the math pack's `mathematical-rigor`, a proved/cited line
  for any non-obvious step.
